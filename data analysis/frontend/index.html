How risk is measured

Risk level uses annualized volatility: LOW below 15%, MEDIUM 15% to 30%, HIGH above 30%. It moves up one level if the maximum drawdown is worse than -25%.

VaR is the loss that should not be exceeded on a single day with the chosen confidence. Historical VaR uses the real return percentile. Parametric VaR assumes normally distributed returns.

Maximum drawdown is the largest fall from a previous peak to a later low.
